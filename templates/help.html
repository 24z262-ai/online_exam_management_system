How can we help?

Quick answers about using ExamDesk.

FOR STUDENTS

Examinations & results

How do I start an examination?＋

Sign in, open an available examination from your dashboard, review its details, and select Start Examination. The timer begins when the exam opens.

Can I submit more than once?＋

Each student can submit one attempt for each examination. Review your answers before submitting because attempts cannot be restarted.

When will I see my score?＋

Your result is calculated automatically and shown after you submit. You can revisit it from your dashboard or Score Analysis page.

What does the score analysis show?＋

It summarizes completed exams, your average and best score, the score trend over time, and how your results fall into score ranges.

What happens when the timer reaches zero?＋

The examination submits automatically. The scheduled end time also closes attempts.

FOR ADMINISTRATORS

Building & publishing exams

How do I finish and publish an examination?＋

Save the exam details, add questions, then choose Finish Editing to return to the exam list or Publish Examination to make it visible to students. Publishing requires at least one question.

Can I fix a question after adding it?＋

Yes. Open Examinations, choose Questions for the exam, and select Edit beside the question. You can update its wording, options, and correct answer.

How do start and end times work?＋

Set the availability window in the exam details. Students can view a published exam before it opens, but can only start during its window. The scheduled end time also limits the attempt.

Can I unpublish an exam?＋

Yes. Open the exam’s question page and choose Unpublish. Students can no longer start it until you publish it again.
